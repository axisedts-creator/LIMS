Problem: 03-APP-FLOW s11.1 line 313 says `dr-promote.yml (pg_ctl promote; archive to repo2 continues from dr-1)`. 02-TRD s9 (line 733) says "repo2's host (dr-1) never holds any cipher pass", and T-12 built the standby with no pgBackRest and archive_mode off.   (b) amend 02-TRD s9 to allow the repo2 pass on dr-1's tmpfs after a promotion only, and add repo2-* (pass from the secrets store, tmpfs include) as a second repo in dr-promote.yml.
