The CR-C9-005 index `lab.qc_results (tenant_id, created_at, id)` then cannot supply the order. Every page becomes a sort of the whole remaining backlog; on a tenant's first sync (cursor −400 days, §6.2 step 1) that is O(backlog) per page.    `SELECT ${VIEW_COLS} FROM (SELECT * FROM lab.v_pub_qc_result WHERE <same predicate> ORDER BY created_at, qc_result_id LIMIT $4) q ORDER BY q.created_at, q.qc_result_id`

2. services/api/src/modules/quality/service/observation.service.ts:109 (`lockPair` inside `importPage`). §6.2 step 4 says to lock each (instrument, analyte) group "in its own transaction".
