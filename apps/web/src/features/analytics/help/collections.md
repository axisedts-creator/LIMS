# Collections history

## Purpose
Shows money received over time, by payment method, staff member, centre or day. Use it to review cash handling.

## Metrics
- **Collected (M25)**: payments received on the date they were recorded.
- **Reversed/refunded (M26)**: payments reversed or refunded on that date, shown as a positive number.
- **Net collected (M27)**: collected minus reversed/refunded.
- **Payment and reversal entries (M28, M29)**: counts.

Cash basis: this includes payments of earlier invoices, so it differs from income. Collections and Income (M12) will not match on a given day. That is expected.

If you may read the cash report, a link takes you to today's cash report and session close.

## Periods and business day
Group by Method, Staff, Staff × method, Centre, Day, A.D. month or B.S. month. The day is the Nepal-time business day (UTC+05:45). Periods show A.D. and B.S.

## Freshness
"Updated {time}" shows the last calculation. "Data may be out of date" means changes are still being processed.

## Centre scope
You only see centres you have access to. Payments with no centre, such as credit-party settlements, appear as "No centre (credit parties)" and only for users with access to all centres.
