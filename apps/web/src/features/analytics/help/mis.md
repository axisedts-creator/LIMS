# MIS catalogue reports and Bill Of The Day

## Purpose
The MIS catalogue reports (Sales representative, Consultant, Collection boy, Repeated patient, Repeated test, IPD and OPD, Party billing, Outsource and inter lab transfer, and the two reports behind the referral report switch) and Bill Of The Day open from MIS Report. Each report has its own filters, a Print button, a CSV export and a Schedule link.

## Fields
- **Columns**: the column headers of each report are always shown, also when there is nothing to show. Money columns are in rupees.
- **Filters**: only the filters of that report are shown (period, centre, and where the report has them party, representative, consultant, collector, department, patient type or minimum visits).
- **Export**: the CSV file is built on the server with the filters on screen and appears in My exports.
- **Schedule**: opens Scheduled reports, where the report can be sent by email on a schedule.
- **Bill Of The Day**: one business day, the bill list and the totals by payment mode, printable on one page.

## Periods and business day
A business day runs 00:00–24:00 Nepal time (UTC+05:45). Dates are A.D. calendar dates; the export summary shows the period in A.D. and B.S. where the server provides it.

## Freshness
The rows come from the export and the schedule while the on screen read is not yet available. "Updated {time}" on other reports shows the last calculation.

## Centre scope
You only see figures for centres you have access to. Users with access to all centres see every centre.
