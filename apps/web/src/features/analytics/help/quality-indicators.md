# Quality indicators

## Purpose
Shows sample rejections and how quickly critical values were communicated.

## Metrics
- **Rejections (M70)**: samples rejected in the period, counted on the rejection date.
- **Recollections ordered (M71)**: rejections that led to a new collection.
- **Specimens received (M73)**: samples received at the lab. Aliquots are not counted.
- **Rejections per 100 received (M72)**: rejections × 100 ÷ specimens received, one decimal. Shows "—" when nothing was received.
- **Critical values (M75 to M78)**: detected, notified (reached and read back), median time to notify and maximum time to notify. The median is shown per day only.

Group rejections by Reason, Centre, Specimen type, Day, A.D. month or B.S. month.

## Periods and business day
A business day runs 00:00–24:00 Nepal time (UTC+05:45). Periods show both A.D. and B.S. dates. Rates are computed from sums over the period, not by averaging daily rates.

## Freshness
"Updated {time} (Nepal time)" shows the last calculation. "Data may be out of date" means changes are still being processed.

## Centre scope
You only see centres you have access to. Users with access to all centres see all centres.
