# Referral business report

## Purpose
Orders and billing by referring doctor, for internal review only. Viewing this report is recorded. It may be switched off for your lab, and it asks you to verify with your authenticator app before it opens.

## Metrics
- **Referred orders, patient visits, tests ordered (M30 to M32)**: as in Top referrers. Patient visits are patient-days.
- **Net billed (M33)**: cash invoices (net) plus credit-party charges (gross minus discount, before tax) minus credit notes. Credit-party work is counted on the day it was charged.

Net billed is not equal to the referrer's share of Income (M12), so the two will differ. The SELF row has no referrer, so Net billed shows "—" there.

This report shows no rate, no amount due and no payment. Do not treat the figures as a statement of what anyone is owed. Exports of this report are also recorded.

## Periods and business day
Default period: Last B.S. month. Group by Referrer, Referrer × A.D. month or Referrer × B.S. month. A business day runs 00:00–24:00 Nepal time (UTC+05:45). Periods show A.D. and B.S. dates.

## Freshness
"Updated {time}" shows the last calculation. "Data may be out of date" means changes are still being processed.

## Centre scope
You only see centres you have access to. Users with access to all centres see all centres.
