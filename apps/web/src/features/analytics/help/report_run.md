# Report run (from a scheduled email)

## Purpose
The page you reach by clicking the link in a scheduled report email. You must sign in first. It shows the reports of that schedule for the period named in the title.

## Fields
- **Title**: schedule name and the period, in A.D. and B.S. dates.
- **Report cards**: one card for each report in the schedule, using the same tables and charts as on the report pages. The filters are read-only and show the run's period and scope.
- **Notice**: "Figures as of now for this period. Your access limits apply."

Reports you may not see are left out, and so are figures for centres you cannot access. Business days are counted in Nepal time (UTC+05:45).

If you see "This report link is not for your account or has expired", the link was sent to someone else or is too old. Ask the owner of the schedule, or open the report from the Analytics menu.

## Freshness
The figures are calculated when you open the page, not when the email was sent, so they can differ from what you expected. The header shows "Updated {time}". If it says "Data may be out of date", changes are still being processed.

## Access
Only the schedule's recipients and its owner can open a run. The email itself contains no figures, only the link.
