# Top centres

## Purpose
Ranks collection centres by activity, so you can compare centres side by side.

## Metrics
- **Centre orders, patient visits, tests ordered (M35 to M37)**: counts per centre, split by how the sample was collected: walk-in, home, centre or partner. Patient visits count patient-days, not distinct persons.
- **Centre net sales (M38)**: Income (M12) grouped by centre.

Controls: sort by orders, net sales, patient visits or tests ordered; limit 10, 20 or 50.

## Periods and business day
A business day runs 00:00–24:00 Nepal time (UTC+05:45). The header shows the period in A.D. and B.S. Choose a preset such as This B.S. month, or a custom range of up to 366 days.

## Freshness
"Updated {time}" shows when figures were last calculated. "Data may be out of date" means recent changes are still being processed, so the ranking can move.

## Centre scope
You only see centres you have access to. A user with a single centre sees just that centre. Users with access to all centres see every centre in the ranking.
