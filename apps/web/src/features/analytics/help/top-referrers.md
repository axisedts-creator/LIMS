# Top referrers

## Purpose
Ranks referring doctors by the work they send to the lab. There is no money column here. Net billed per referrer is in the Referral business report only.

## Metrics
- **Referred orders (M30)**: non-cancelled orders from the referrer.
- **Referred patient visits (M31)**: patient-days, as for Patient visits (M04). The same person can be counted more than once over several days.
- **Referred tests ordered (M32)**: tests on those orders.
- **SELF (no referrer)**: orders without a referring doctor are grouped on one row labelled SELF.

Controls: sort by orders, patient visits or tests ordered; limit 10, 20, 50 or 100; search for a doctor (at least 2 letters). "Combine merged doctors" adds records of the same doctor together; it is on by default.

## Periods and business day
A business day runs 00:00–24:00 Nepal time (UTC+05:45). The period shows both A.D. and B.S. dates. Choose a preset or a custom range of up to 366 days.

## Freshness
"Updated {time} (Nepal time)" shows the last calculation. "Data may be out of date" means changes are still being processed.

## Centre scope
You only see orders from centres you have access to. Users with access to all centres see the whole lab.
