# Credit parties

## Purpose
List the organisations that buy on credit, such as hospitals, companies and referring clinics. Use it to see each party's limit, how much is outstanding, and whether the party is locked from new credit charges.

## Fields
- **Code** – the short identifier of the party.
- **Name** – click to open the party's page.
- **Limit** – the credit limit in NPR.
- **Used** – the amount currently outstanding.
- **Credit days** – the number of days allowed before a charge is overdue.
- **Lock** – Open, or Locked with the reason. A locked party cannot be given new credit charges until it is unlocked on its page. If automatic locking is turned on in Billing settings, a party is locked after its credit days pass with an unpaid balance.

Invoices raised against a credit party follow the same rule as every other invoice: they cannot be edited or deleted, only cancelled with a reason, which issues a credit note.

## Shortcuts
