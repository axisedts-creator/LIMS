# Daily cash

## Purpose
Reconcile the cash taken on one business date and close your own shift. The screen shows collections by staff member and payment method, and the shifts opened and closed that day, with the counted cash compared to the expected cash.

## Fields
- **Business date** – the day being shown. Change it to look at an earlier day.
- **Staff / Method** – who collected, and by which payment method.
- **Entries / Reversals** – number of payments taken and number reversed.
- **Gross / Reversed / Net** – money taken, money reversed, and the difference, in NPR.
- **Shifts: Opened / Closed** – when each shift started and ended; an open shift shows Open.
- **Expected cash / Counted cash / Variance** – what the system expects in the drawer, what the staff member counted, and the difference. A variance above the limit set in Billing settings raises an alert for finance.
- **My shift – Note** – optional text (up to 500 characters) saved with the shift when you close it.

Payments are never edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected by a reversal, which appears in the Reversals column and in the audit trail.

## Shortcuts
- `?` – list all shortcuts on this screen.
- `Ctrl+Enter` – close your shift from the shift form.
- `Esc` – cancel the form.
