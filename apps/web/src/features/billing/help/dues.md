# Dues

## Purpose
See which registrations still have money to collect. Each row is one registration with a balance due after payments and credit notes. Use it to chase unpaid balances and to open the registration to take a payment.

## Fields
- **Registration no** – click to open the registration on its Billing tab.
- **Patient** – the patient shown on the registration.
- **Centre** – the collection centre of the registration.
- **Invoiced** – total of invoices issued for the registration.
- **Paid** – payments received.
- **Credited** – credit notes issued (cancellations and partial credits).
- **Due** – invoiced minus paid minus credited.
- **Oldest invoice age** – days since the oldest unpaid invoice was issued.

A registration with nothing left to collect does not appear here. A cancelled invoice reduces the balance through its credit note; the invoice itself is never edited or deleted.
