# Invoices

## Purpose
Find and open every invoice your lab has issued. Use it to look up an invoice by number, date, buyer or status, and to open the invoice to print it, cancel it or issue a credit note.

Invoices cannot be edited or deleted. To correct an invoice, cancel it with a reason; a credit note is issued automatically. Invoice numbers run in an unbroken sequence per fiscal year, so a cancelled invoice stays in the list and keeps its number.

## Fields
- **Invoice no** – the number printed on the invoice. Click it to open the invoice.
- **B.S. date / A.D. date** – the issue date in both calendars.
- **Buyer** – the name on the invoice, or a dash when none was entered.
- **Net** – the invoice total in NPR.
- **Status** – Issued or Cancelled.
- **Prints** – how many times the invoice has been printed. Reprints are marked as copies.
- **CBMS** – whether the invoice has been sent to the IRD's CBMS. Until the connection is set up the label reads "Not sent — CBMS connection not configured".
- **Filters** – fiscal year (B.S., for example 2083/84), date range, centre, status, invoice no and credit party.

## Shortcuts
- `?` – list all shortcuts on this screen.
