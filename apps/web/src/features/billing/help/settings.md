# Billing settings

## Purpose
Set how your lab's billing behaves: numbering, tax display, optional features and alert limits. Only people with the settings permission can change them. Changes apply to documents issued afterwards; invoices already issued are never altered.

## Fields
- **Invoice series** – One series for the lab. Numbers run without gaps within each fiscal year.
- **Prices include tax** – whether entered prices already contain VAT.
- **Abbreviated invoices** – allow the short invoice form for small sales, where your tax rules permit it.
- **Pay by QR** – show a payment QR code on the invoice.
- **Receipt numbering** – give receipts their own numbers.
- **Lock credit parties automatically after the credit days** – lock a party when its balance stays unpaid past its credit days.
- **Cash variance alert** – the difference in NPR between counted and expected cash that raises an alert.
- **Refund alert (per day)** – the number of refunds in a day that raises an alert.

Whether CBMS is required for your lab is a tax-registration setting managed by your administrator, not on this page. The CBMS screen shows the current sending status.

## Shortcuts
- `?` – list all shortcuts on this screen.
- `Ctrl+Enter` – save settings.
- `Tab` – move to the next setting.
