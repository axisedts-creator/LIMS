# Finance worklist

## Purpose
Work through the items that need a finance decision: credit notes still to be raised, payments that need review, and refunds waiting for approval. Each tab is a queue; clear it from the top.

## Fields
- **Credit notes required** – invoices that must be corrected with a credit note. Open the invoice and cancel it with a reason or issue a partial credit; the item leaves the queue once the credit note exists. Invoices themselves cannot be edited or deleted.
- **Payments to review** – payments that need a finance decision. Choose Resolve, then pick Refunded, Kept as advance or Other. A note is required for Other.
- **Refund approvals** – refund requests from staff. Approve or Reject. You cannot approve a request you made yourself. Reject requires a note.
- **Actions** – buttons are disabled when you do not have the approval permission.

Every decision is saved with your name and time and shows in the audit trail.

## Shortcuts
- `?` – list all shortcuts on this screen.
- `Left` / `Right` – move between the three queues.
- `Ctrl+Enter` – submit the Reject or Resolve dialog.
- `Esc` – close the dialog without saving.
