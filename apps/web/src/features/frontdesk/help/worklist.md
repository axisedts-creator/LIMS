# Front-desk worklist

## Purpose
One place for registration follow-ups. The screen is at `/frontdesk/worklist`. Use the tabs to see what needs attention today.

## Fields
- **Recollection needed** – orders where a new sample must be collected. Columns: registration number, patient, created time.
- **Booking cancelled after registration** – orders whose booking was cancelled after you registered them.
- **Pre-registered today** – booked orders waiting for the patient to arrive. Open one to confirm it.
- **Possible duplicates created today** – patients created today who look like an existing patient (shared phone). Shown only if you may register patients. Open both records and compare before acting.
- **Resolve** – on the first two tabs, choose how the flag was resolved. Needs permission to edit orders.

## Shortcuts
- `Up` / `Down` – move between rows.
- `Enter` – open the order (or patient on the duplicates tab).
- `R` – resolve the focused flag row.
- `?` – list the shortcuts available on this screen.
