# Home visits (dispatch board)

## Purpose
Shows every home visit for one day and one centre. Visits that nobody has taken yet are on the left under **Unassigned**. Each phlebotomist's route is a card on the right, in the order they will visit.

## The steps
1. Pick the **Date** and the **Centre** at the top. Press **Refresh** to see the latest.
2. For an unassigned visit, open the **⋯** menu on its row and choose **Schedule** to set the time window, the address and a note for the phlebotomist.
3. Choose **Assign** to give the visit to a phlebotomist. The list shows how many visits each person already has that day. A route that is full cannot be chosen. For a booking, assigning also registers the order and issues the bill in front desk.
4. To change the order on a route, use **Move up** and **Move down** on a row, then press **Save order**. The phone shows the new order after its next sync.
5. **Unassign** takes a visit off a route and gives a reason. **Cancel** closes a visit and also needs a reason. Cancelling does not cancel the order or bill in front desk.
6. **Print labels** opens the tube labels for the visit's order. You only see it if your role allows printing labels.

## The note for the phlebotomist
The note is shown on the phlebotomist's phone. Do not write test names, results or diagnoses in it.

## Needs attention
Turn on **Needs attention** to see only visits with an open flag. Every flag is written in words, for example "Address needed" or "Collection on hold".

## Keyboard
- **Alt+D** goes to the date field, **Alt+R** refreshes.
- On an unassigned row, **Alt+S** schedules it and **Alt+A** assigns it. **Enter** on the row opens Assign when the visit already has a time, otherwise Schedule.
- **Ctrl+Enter** submits an open dialog and **Esc** closes it.
- The order of a route is changed with buttons only, so it works without a mouse.
