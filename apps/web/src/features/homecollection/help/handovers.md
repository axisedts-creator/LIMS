# Tube handovers

## Purpose
When a phlebotomist brings collected tubes to a centre, the tubes are handed over here. This page lists the handovers for one centre so staff can check each tube against what the phlebotomist says they carried.

## The steps
1. Pick the **Status** and the **Centre** at the top. The list starts on **Waiting**: handovers nobody has checked yet.
2. Press **Open** on a row. A panel shows the tubes in that handover: barcode and specimen type.
3. **Scan** each tube with the barcode scanner, or type the barcode by hand. The tube is ticked as received and the count at the top goes up. Scanning a tube twice changes nothing.
4. If a scanned barcode is not in this handover, the panel says so and nothing is ticked. You can also tick or untick a tube by hand.
5. Press **Confirm handover**. If every tube is ticked, the handover is closed as **Received**.
6. If some tubes are not ticked, you are asked to mark them as missing. The handover then shows **Missing tubes**, and the dispatcher is told so they can follow up.

## What you can and cannot see
The panel shows tubes only. It does not show patients, visits or addresses, so you do not need access to home visits to do this job.

## After it is confirmed
A handled handover opens read-only. It shows who received it, when, and whether each tube was received or missing. It cannot be changed from here.

## Keyboard
- The scan box is ready as soon as the panel opens, so you can start scanning straight away.
- **Ctrl+Enter** confirms the handover. **Esc** closes the panel.
