# Home Collection settings

## Purpose
One page for the rules home collection follows in your lab. Change what you need and press **Save settings**, or press **Ctrl+Enter** from any field. Every value is checked before it is sent.

## Sync & privacy
- **Sync interval** is how often a phone asks for changes, in seconds. **Clear phone data after offline** is how many hours a phone may stay offline before it erases its data.
- **Capture GPS point** is switched off and not available yet: it needs the location component, which is pending. **GPS kept for** is how many days a captured point is kept.
- **Allow maps hand-off** lets the phone open its own maps app. Only the visit address is sent, never a name, tests or phone number.
- **Minimum app version** is written like 1.10.0. Phones below it are shown as **Update required** on the phones page.

## Visit checks
- **Second identifier** is the second thing a phlebotomist checks with the patient. Phone number works today. Date of birth is not available yet.
- **Allow typed barcodes** lets staff type a tube barcode when the scanner fails.
- **Consent text** is what the phlebotomist reads out before collecting. Change the **Consent text version** whenever you change the text.

## Payments
Choose from the six payment methods in the list, at most once each, between 1 and 10 methods. Give each a label and say whether a reference is needed.

## Handover
**Confirmation at centre required** makes tubes wait for a check at the centre. **Overdue after** is how many hours before an unconfirmed handover is flagged.

## Routes & messages
**Max visits per route** limits one phlebotomist's day. Staff SMS are not sent yet (Phase 1). **SMS to patient on scheduling** is not available until its template is built (pending). **Clock tolerance** is how many seconds a phone's clock may be off.

If someone else saved settings first, a banner asks you to reload and try again.
