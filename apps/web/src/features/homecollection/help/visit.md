# Home visit

## Purpose
Shows one home visit: who the patient is, where and when the visit is, which tubes were collected, what was paid, and anything that needs attention. Open it from the dispatch board with **Open** on a row.

## The cards
- **Patient and address** is only shown if your role can assign visits. The phone number is hidden as "••••". Press **Show phone** to see it. Every time you show a phone number it is logged: who looked, which visit and when. Show it only when you need to call the patient.
- **Schedule** gives the time window, the route day, the phlebotomist and when the phone last synced.
- **Tubes** lists each tube with its lab status. The Collection column says **Applied**, **On hold** or **Discarded**. "Typed — not verified" means the barcode was typed by hand and not scanned.
- **On hold** appears when a tube was collected but could not be accepted automatically. The reason is written next to it, for example the visit changed before the phone uploaded it.
- **Payments** shows what the phlebotomist took. **Open billing** goes to the order in front desk.
- **Needs attention** lists the flags on this visit in words. **Resolve** closes a flag and asks for a reason.
- **Timeline** is the history of the visit, oldest first.

## Reviewing a tube on hold
1. Press **Review** on the row.
2. Choose **Apply** to accept the tube as collected, or **Discard** to throw it away. Apply is not offered when the scanned barcode did not match the tube, or after the system says the tube changed. Then only Discard remains.
3. Pick a reason and press **Confirm**. If you pick Other, say what happened.

## Revisit
**Create revisit** is offered on a failed visit, or on a completed visit where only some tubes were collected. It makes a new unassigned visit and opens it.

## Other buttons
**Schedule**, **Assign** or **Reassign**, **Unassign** and **Cancel visit** work as on the dispatch board. They are hidden once a visit is closed. **Print labels** needs the label printing permission.
