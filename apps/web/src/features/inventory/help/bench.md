# Bench

For technologists: open reagent containers, finish or discard them, and log calibrations or manual QC runs.

- Open a container: scan or search the item, pick the lot, optionally pick the analyser, and set when it was opened.
- Finish or discard a container; for discard enter the remaining quantity and a reason.
- Log calibration or manual QC: choose kind, analyser, test, analyte, number of observations (QC), time and reason.
- Choose the analyser this bottle is loaded on. Automatic deductions for that analyser use this lot.
- Note field: Do not enter patient details.

- After saving you see "Logged. {n} items deducted." or "Logged. No consumption rule matches, so nothing was deducted."
