# Purchase orders

Create, approve, send and close purchase orders. Draft orders are edited in a full editor; other orders show a timeline.

- Choose "New PO", pick supplier and receiving store, add lines with item, pack and quantity. Amounts are calculated by the system when you save.
- Press "Save draft", then "Approve" (needs inventory.po.approve; disabled while unsaved changes exist).
- Use "Mark as sent" and choose the channel, then "Receive" when goods arrive. "Close" ends a partly received order.
- "Print" opens a print view; drafts carry the watermark "DRAFT - NOT APPROVED".

- Approving your own PO is allowed only if Settings says so.
