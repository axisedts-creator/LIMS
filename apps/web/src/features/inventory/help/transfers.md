# Transfers

Move stock between stores. Stock leaves the sender on dispatch and shows as in transit until the other store receives it.

- Choose "New transfer", pick From and To stores and add lines (item, lot or auto, quantity).
- Dispatch and confirm: stock leaves the From store now and shows as in transit.
- The receiving store enters the received quantity per line; if less, choose a discrepancy reason.

- The To store can be any active store of the lab, even outside your branch.
