# Wastage, adjustments and expiry write-off

Three tabs: Wastage, Adjustment and Expiry write-off. Each change needs a coded reason.

- Wastage: store, time, reason, optional note (500 characters at most) and lines.
- Adjustment (needs stock adjust permission): reason, direction in or out per line. Step-up sign-in may be asked.
- Expiry write-off: choose expired lots (200 at most) and confirm "Write off all remaining stock of {n} expired lots?".

- Wastage is reported separately and is not counted in cost per test.
