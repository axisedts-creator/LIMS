# Critical values

## Purpose

Track critical results and record who was told, how and when. Every notification is kept as a permanent record.

## Steps

1. Open the list. Open rows come first; "age since detected" counts live.
2. Open a row to see the value, limits, patient, referrer and the timeline of earlier notifications.
3. Fill in the form: notified party, name, contact method, notified at, outcome. Tick "Read-back confirmed" only if the person repeated the value, and then enter the read-back text (1 to 300 characters).
4. Press save. The status moves from Open to Notified.

## Shortcuts

- Tab and Shift+Tab move between form fields
- Enter opens the focused row in the list

## Error messages

- "No critical values." — nothing to follow up.
- "Notification recorded." — success.
- "Entries cannot be edited or deleted." — shown under the form; check before saving.
- Notified at cannot be more than 5 minutes in the future.
- A new critical value that appears while you are on a screen is announced immediately.
- "You don't have access to this screen." — your role does not include this screen. There is no Retry button; ask your administrator.
- "Something went wrong" — "Please try again. If it keeps happening, give this reference to support: {request_id}". Press Retry; if it repeats, give the reference to support.
- "This item doesn't exist or isn't visible to you." — the link is wrong or the item belongs to another organisation. The message is the same in both cases.
