# Status board

## Purpose

Follow every registration from waiting to printed, in one table that refreshes itself.

## Steps

1. Pick a date range (required, at most 7 days). Optionally pick a centre or status chips.
2. Read the status: Waiting, Collected, In lab, Ready, Printed, Printed (partial), Printed (preliminary). A "Sample rejected" badge and an urgent mark may also show.
3. The board refreshes every 30 s and shows "Updated {time}". Use the pause toggle to stop refreshing while you read.

## Shortcuts

- Tab and Shift+Tab move between filters
- Enter or Space toggles the pause switch when it has focus

## Error messages

- "No registrations in this period." — widen the date range.
- The date range must not be longer than 7 days.
- "You don't have access to this screen." — your role does not include this screen. There is no Retry button; ask your administrator.
- "Something went wrong" — "Please try again. If it keeps happening, give this reference to support: {request_id}". Press Retry; if it repeats, give the reference to support.
- "This item doesn't exist or isn't visible to you." — the link is wrong or the item belongs to another organisation. The message is the same in both cases.
