# Micro settings

## Purpose

Configure microbiology for your laboratory: general options, dictionaries, AST panels, instrument and code mappings, and plating protocols. The page is at /micro/settings. Changing anything needs the permission micro.config.manage.

## Steps

1. Open a tab: General, Dictionaries, Panels, Mappings or Protocols.
2. Pick an item from the list. Its details open in the drawer on the right.
3. Edit the section and press Save for that section. Each section saves on its own.
4. If someone else changed the item first, reload the item and apply your change again.

## Notes

- Dictionaries (organisms, antibiotics, media and so on) start from a global list. Your additions and overrides apply to your laboratory only.
- The manual AST standard in General sets the default for hand-entered results. Instrument defaults can be set per instrument in Mappings.
- Deactivate an item instead of deleting it; old reports keep showing it.

## Error messages

- "You don't have access to this screen." — you can read but not change settings, or your role does not include microbiology. Ask your administrator.
