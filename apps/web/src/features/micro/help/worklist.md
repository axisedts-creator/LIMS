# Microbiology worklist

## Purpose

See every specimen and culture that needs microbiology attention, grouped by stage, and open the culture page to work on it. The page is at /micro/worklist. Shortcut: press g u to jump here from anywhere.

## Steps

1. Pick a tab: Awaiting setup, Incubating, Growth / work-up, Amending or Recently submitted. The count on each tab shows how many items are waiting.
2. Narrow the list with the filters (status, centre, received date). The received range can be at most 31 days.
3. To start a new culture, scan the specimen barcode (Alt+B focuses the scan box). Choose the culture type if asked.
4. Press Enter on a row to open the culture page.

## Tabs

- Awaiting setup — specimens received but no culture started yet.
- Incubating — plates are in the incubator; the next read due time decides the order.
- Growth / work-up — growth was seen; isolates need identification or AST.
- Amending — a final report was reopened and a correction is in progress.
- Recently submitted — reports sent in the last few days.

## Error messages

- "You don't have access to this screen." — your role does not include microbiology. Ask your administrator.
- "We couldn't look that up. Try again." — the barcode lookup failed; scan again.
- Empty tabs show a short note such as "No cultures incubating." This is not an error.
