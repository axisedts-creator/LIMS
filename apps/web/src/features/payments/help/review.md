# Payment review

## Purpose
Lists online payments (eSewa, Khalti) that need a person to look at them. A payment is "Under review" (held) when the provider's answer did not match what we expected, for example the amount was different or the answer was unclear. Nothing here is marked paid until the provider confirms it.

## Filters
- **Status**: starts on "Under review". Other choices are Waiting for provider, Started, Failed, Expired and Paid.
- **Gateway**: eSewa or Khalti. Fonepay is listed but not available yet.
- **Hold reason**: why the payment was held.
- **Created**: the dates to look at. It starts on the last 7 days and can cover up to 31 days.
- **Centre**: shown when you can see more than one centre.

Press **Alt+R** to jump to "Reset filters".

## Columns
Created, Gateway, Amount, Status, Reason, Last checked, Order and Intent. The status is always written in words. "Paid" appears only when the provider has confirmed the payment and the server has recorded it.

## What to do
Open a row (press Enter, or use the Intent link) to see the full history. From there you can re-check with the provider, and, if you have permission, mark the payment as paid or failed. A payment can only be marked as paid when the provider confirms it with the exact amount.

If the list is empty, no payment needs a person right now.
