# Fonepay QR worksheet

## Purpose
Fonepay live payments are not available yet. Until they are, patients may pay at the counter with a Fonepay QR code, and the payment is recorded by hand. This worksheet compares what was recorded for a business date with your bank statement, so finance can see any gap.

## How to fill it in
1. Choose the **Business date** (it starts on yesterday and cannot be in the future) and the centre, or All centres.
2. Read the **System total**: the sum of the Fonepay QR payments recorded for that date, with the number of entries. It cannot be edited.
3. Type the **Bank statement total** for the same date and the **Statement reference** from the bank statement.
4. The **Difference** is the statement total minus the system total. If it is not zero, write a note that explains it (for example a bank fee). The note is required.
5. Press **Save worksheet**. A date and centre can be saved once; if it was already saved you will be told.

## The list
Below the form you see saved worksheets: date, centre, system total, statement total, difference, status (Matched or Accepted with difference) and who saved it. This page only helps you compare numbers; it does not confirm any payment with the provider.
