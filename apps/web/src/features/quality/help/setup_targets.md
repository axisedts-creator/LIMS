# QC set-up: Targets

## Purpose
A target is the mean and standard deviation (SD) that QC values of one lot are compared with.

## Fields
- **Mean** and **SD**: typed as numbers with the decimals of the analyte plus two. SD is greater than zero.
- **Source**: Manufacturer (typed in) or Established (calculated from your own accepted QC values).

## Rules
Mean and SD of a saved target never change. To correct one, activate a new target; the old one is superseded and keeps its dates. A draft can be discarded, an active target can be retired with a reason.
