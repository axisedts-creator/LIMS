# Report template

## Purpose
Edit the draft of a report template. Save the draft, check it with Preview with sample data, then publish it. New reports use the published version; reports that were already made keep the layout they were made with.

## Fields
- **Page**: size and margins in millimetres.
- **Letterhead**: header and footer images, and the space kept free on plain paper (0 to 80 mm top and bottom).
- **Header fields**: which patient and order details appear above the results.
- **Results**: method line, units column, grouping by package, page breaks, and department approval lines.
- **Signatories**: where signatories come from, how many (1 to 4), signature images, and the registration label (up to 20 characters).
- **QR code**: on or off, size 15 to 35 mm, caption up to 60 characters.
- **Watermark**: text up to 40 characters, opacity 0.05 to 0.3.
- **Disclaimers**: up to five lines of up to 200 characters.
- **Fonts**: base size 7 to 11 pt.
- Without the template manage permission the form is read-only.

## Shortcuts
This screen has no page shortcuts. Use Tab and Enter; Esc closes a dialog.
