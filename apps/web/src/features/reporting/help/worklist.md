# Reports

## Purpose
The reports worklist shows every report that is waiting for something, ready to go out, held, or already released. Use the tabs to work through the day: awaiting content, ready to release, held (dues), render failed, released today, and all. The Critical alerts panel above the tabs lists critical results from the last 24 hours and who was told. It never shows a result value or a patient name.

## Fields
- **Reg no**: opens the report. **Patient**, **Centre**, **Family** (Lab or Narrative).
- **Items**: ready items out of all items, for example 7/9.
- **Status**: the report status. Held reports show the amount due.
- **Version**: the latest version, for example v2. **Registered** and **Updated** are the order and last change times.
- **Registration no. or barcode**: type or scan. One match opens the report. Barcode search needs the lab specimen read permission; without it, enter the registration no.
- **Release N**: appears when rows are selected on the Ready to release or Held tabs and you may bulk release. Reports with dues stay held unless an override exists.

## Shortcuts
- `Alt+1` to `Alt+6`: switch between the six tabs.
- `/`: focus the registration no. or barcode search.
- `Alt+R`: release the selected reports (opens a confirmation). Only available with the bulk release permission.
