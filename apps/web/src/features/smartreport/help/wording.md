# Wording terms

## Purpose

Wording terms are blocked words and phrases. A library text that contains one of them fails the checks, so it cannot be submitted. They keep patient wording safe, for example by stopping diagnostic language.

## Columns

Term, Language, Scope, Reason, Active.

- **Scope**: Global terms apply to every lab and are managed by the platform. Global rows have no actions. This lab terms are yours and can be changed.
- **Language**: English, Nepali or Any.

## Actions

- **Add term**: enter the Term (2 to 60 characters), choose the Language and give a Reason (up to 200 characters) so others know why it is blocked.
- **Deactivate**: use the row action on a term of This lab and confirm. The term stops blocking text from then on. Existing approved versions are not changed.

## Good to know

New terms apply the next time checks run. Run checks again on any draft that may be affected.
