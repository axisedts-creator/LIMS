# How the app protects your reports

Your reports are private. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does to keep them that way and what stays your job.

## Your sign-in is kept in protected storage

After you sign in, the app keeps your sign-in in the secure storage of your phone (the Keychain on iPhone, the Keystore on Android). It does not keep your reports on the phone. A report is downloaded when you open it and the temporary file is deleted afterwards.

## Screens are protected

Report screens are hidden in the list of recent apps and cannot be captured by a screenshot. This is on purpose. If you need a copy, open the report and choose to save it as a PDF. The app shows a warning first, because a saved PDF is outside the app and anyone with your phone could open it.

## A code works once, on one phone

The lab gives you a code after checking your identity. The code works once and only on the phone number it was issued for. After you use it, the profile is linked to your account. You only see profiles that are linked to you. Linking a child or a relative needs the lab to check that person as well.

## Remove a profile any time

Open Profiles, choose the profile and Remove. The reports stay with the lab, but you no longer see them in the app. To bring the profile back, ask the lab for a new code.

## If your phone is lost or stolen

Ask the lab to revoke the app link of each of your profiles. The lost phone can then no longer open those reports. On your new phone, sign in with your number and ask the lab for a new code. Use a screen lock on your phone as well.

## Payments and legal pages open in the system browser

Online payment and the legal pages open in your system browser, not inside the app, so you can check the address and the lock sign before you enter anything. The app does not send push messages. It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.

## Privacy policy and Terms of use

Find Privacy policy and Terms of use on the first screen and in About. They open in the system browser. Your lab may also show its own privacy page under its name.
