# Reminders, memberships and enquiries

This page explains three screens. It mirrors the in-app help for `engagement.reminders`, `engagement.memberships` and `engagement.enquiries`.

## Reminders (`/engagement/reminders`)

Help key `engagement.reminders`. Needs `engagement.reminder.read`. The list shows due date, patient, kind, reason, status, sent time and result. A reminder can be held back, and the result says why: no SMS consent, no phone number, SMS turned off, or the patient opted out. To add one, choose the patient, a reason and a due time at least one hour ahead. Opened from a patient, the screen also suggests follow-up reminders.

## Memberships (`/engagement/memberships`)

Help key `engagement.memberships`. Needs `engagement.membership.read`.

1. Lookup and enrol: scan or type a membership card to see the card. To enrol, pick the patient, an active plan and a start date between today and 30 days ahead.
2. Plans: code, name, description, price list and validity in days. Changing plans needs a second sign-in step (aal2); the shell asks for it.

## Enquiries (`/engagement/enquiries`)

Help key `engagement.enquiries`. Needs `engagement.enquiry.read`. The inbox lists the last message, topic, subject and status, with the phone number masked. Open a thread to read it and reply (up to 2000 characters). `Ctrl+Enter` sends and `Alt+X` closes the thread.

Never put test results in a reply. Ask the patient to open the report in the app instead.
