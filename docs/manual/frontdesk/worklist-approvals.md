# Worklist and discount approvals (screens `/frontdesk/worklist` and `/frontdesk/discount-approvals`)

This page explains the daily follow-up screens. It mirrors the in-app help.

## Worklist (`/frontdesk/worklist`)

Tabs: Recollection needed, Booking cancelled after registration, Pre-registered today, and Possible duplicates created today (shown only if you may register patients). Move with the arrow keys, press `Enter` to open a row, and `R` to resolve a flag row; choose how it was resolved.

## Discount approvals (`/frontdesk/discount-approvals`)

For approvers. Pending requests show the time left, the gross, the discount and the reason. Press `A` on a row to approve (after confirming) or `R` to reject (a note is required, up to 500 characters). You cannot approve your own request. Approving needs MFA; if you are asked, open your profile, set it up and try again. The list refreshes about every 10 seconds.
