# Home collection: centre handover guide

This guide is for centre staff who receive tubes from a home-collection phlebotomist. It mirrors the in-app help for the handovers page (`/homecollection/handovers`).

## Why this exists

A phlebotomist brings the tubes they collected and hands them over at a centre. You check each tube against what the phlebotomist says they carried, so a tube that went missing on the way is noticed the same day.

## Steps

1. Pick the **Status** and the **Centre** at the top. The list starts on **Waiting**: handovers nobody has checked yet.
2. Press **Open** on a row. A panel shows the tubes in the handover: barcode and specimen type.
3. **Scan** each tube with the barcode scanner, or type the barcode if the scanner fails. The tube is ticked as received and the count goes up. Scanning the same tube twice changes nothing.
4. If a scanned barcode is not in this handover, the panel says so and nothing is ticked. Put that tube aside and tell the dispatcher.
5. Press **Confirm handover**. If every tube is ticked, the handover closes as **Received** and the lab can receive the specimens.
6. If some tubes are not ticked, you are asked to mark them as missing. The handover then shows **Missing tubes**, and the dispatcher is told so they can follow up. A discrepancy is never fixed by ticking a tube you did not see.

## What you can see

The panel shows tubes only. It does not show patients, visits or addresses, so you do not need access to home visits to do this job.

## After confirming

A confirmed handover opens read-only. It shows who received it, when, and whether each tube was received or missing. It cannot be changed from this page.

## Keyboard

The scan box is ready as soon as the panel opens. **Ctrl+Enter** confirms the handover and **Esc** closes the panel.
