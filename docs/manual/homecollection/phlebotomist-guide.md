# Home collection: phlebotomist guide

This guide is for the phlebotomist who uses the phone app on home visits. The phone works with no signal: everything you record is saved on the phone first and sent when a connection returns.

## Start of day

Sign in, then let the app sync once so today's route is on the phone. Your visits are in the order the dispatcher set. Open a visit to see the time window, the address and the dispatcher's note. Use **Navigate** to open your own maps app; only the address is shared, never the patient's name or tests.

## At the patient's home

1. **Arrived**: confirm you are at the visit.
2. **Identity**: check the patient's name and the second identifier the lab chose. Say if it does not match; do not collect on a mismatch.
3. **Consent**: read the consent text aloud and record the answer.
4. **Collect**: scan each tube's barcode. If the scanner fails and your lab allows it, type the barcode.
5. **Payment**: record the payment method and reference if the visit takes payment.
6. **Finish**: choose the outcome. If you could not collect, choose a reason such as patient not home.

Each step shows "Saved on phone". The **Sync** page shows how many changes are not uploaded yet. Sign out is only available when everything has been uploaded, so sync first at the end of the day.

## Handing tubes to a centre

Choose the tubes you carry, pick the centre and press the hand-over button. The centre then confirms each tube. See the centre handover guide.

## Lost or stolen phone

Act at once, because the phone holds patient data.

1. Tell the dispatcher or your supervisor straight away, by another phone. Say when you last synced.
2. The administrator can remove the phone's access and send a **wipe** command from the devices page. The next time the phone reaches the network it erases its data. A phone that never goes online also erases itself after the offline limit set in settings.
3. Anything saved on that phone and not yet synced is lost with it. The dispatcher lists the visits you had collected since your last sync. For each tube already drawn, the lab records a **manual collection** on the specimen collection screen (C5), so the tube is not lost from the system. Visits with no tube drawn are re-scheduled with **Revisit**.
4. Get a new phone from the administrator and register it before you start work again.

## Do not

Do not write patient names or phone numbers anywhere outside the app. Do not take screenshots; the app blocks them on purpose.
