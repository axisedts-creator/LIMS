# Alerts

## Purpose

Lists open alerts such as below reorder level, expiring soon, expired stock on hand, open container past stability, stock below zero, waiting consumption, expired lot in use, stock record mismatch and counts differ from lab. Screen: /inventory/alerts.

## Steps

1. Filter by kind, status or store.
2. Press Acknowledge once someone is dealing with it.

## Tips

- The same alert is not raised twice; Occurrences counts repeats.
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
