# Bench

## Purpose

For technologists: open reagent containers, finish or discard them, and log calibrations or manual QC runs. Screen: /inventory/bench.

## Steps

1. Open a container: scan or search the item, pick the lot, optionally pick the analyser, and set when it was opened.
2. Finish or discard a container; for discard enter the remaining quantity and a reason.
3. Log calibration or manual QC: choose kind, analyser, test, analyte, number of observations (QC), time and reason.
4. Choose the analyser this bottle is loaded on. Automatic deductions for that analyser use this lot.
5. Note field: Do not enter patient details.

## Tips

- After saving you see "Logged. {n} items deducted." or "Logged. No consumption rule matches, so nothing was deducted."
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
