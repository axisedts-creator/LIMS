# Analyser bindings

## Purpose

Links each analyser to the store its reagent is taken from. Shown as the Analysers tab on a store. Screen: reached from the Inventory menu.

## Steps

1. Choose Bind, pick the analyser and the store, and save.
2. You see "Linked. Waiting records for this analyser will be posted shortly."
3. An analyser with no link uses the default consumption store.

## Tips

- One analyser has one store at a time.
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
