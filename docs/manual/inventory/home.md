# Inventory home

## Purpose

Shows what needs attention today: stock below reorder level, lots expiring soon, expired stock on hand and consumption waiting for a store. Screen: /inventory.

## Steps

1. Read the four tiles: Below reorder, Expiring in N days, Expired on hand, Waiting consumption. Each tile opens the matching alerts or waiting list.
2. If the warning banner says "Automatic consumption is off. Stock is not deducted for tests, QC or reruns until a manager turns it on in Settings.", ask a manager to open settings.
3. Check the 10 newest open alerts and the set-up checklist (stores, items, packs, rules, analyser bindings, opening balances, consumption switched on).
4. Use "New PO" or "Receive goods" in the header for the two most common jobs.

## Tips

- The checklist disappears once every step is done.
- How consumption is counted: see [consumption-model.md](consumption-model.md).
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
