# Purchase orders

## Purpose

Create, approve, send and close purchase orders. Draft orders are edited in a full editor; other orders show a timeline. Screen: /inventory/purchase-orders.

## Steps

1. Choose "New PO", pick supplier and receiving store, add lines with item, pack and quantity. Amounts are calculated by the system when you save.
2. Press "Save draft", then "Approve" (needs inventory.po.approve; disabled while unsaved changes exist).
3. Use "Mark as sent" and choose the channel, then "Receive" when goods arrive. "Close" ends a partly received order.
4. "Print" opens a print view; drafts carry the watermark "DRAFT - NOT APPROVED".

## Tips

- Approving your own PO is allowed only if Settings says so.
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
