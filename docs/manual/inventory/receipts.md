# Goods receipt

## Purpose

Record goods that arrive, with lot number and expiry for each line. Start from a PO ("Receive") or choose "New receipt". Screen: /inventory/receipts.

## Steps

1. Pick the store and supplier; enter supplier invoice number, date and total if you have them.
2. Per line enter pack, quantity, lot number (printed on the pack) and expiry. An expired lot cannot be received.
3. If the invoice total differs from the calculated total, a warning shows the difference.
4. Press "Post receipt" and confirm. Stock is added and the receipt can no longer be edited.

## Tips

- Opening balances appear only for users with stock adjust permission.
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
