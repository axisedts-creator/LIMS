# Stock and issue

## Purpose

Shows stock by store and lot, with expiry shown as text ("Expired" or "Expires in n days"). Issue, transfer or write off from a row. Screen: /inventory/stock.

## Steps

1. Choose a store, then filter by category, item, below reorder, expiring within days or show zero.
2. Press Issue on a row. Choose who it is issued to (department, analyser or general) and the quantity; lot "Auto (FEFO)" takes opened containers first, then earliest expiry.
3. Check the "In transit" card for transfers on the way.

## Tips

- Value and average cost columns show only with cost permission.
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
