# Transfers

## Purpose

Move stock between stores. Stock leaves the sender on dispatch and shows as in transit until the other store receives it. Screen: /inventory/transfers.

## Steps

1. Choose "New transfer", pick From and To stores and add lines (item, lot or auto, quantity).
2. Dispatch and confirm: stock leaves the From store now and shows as in transit.
3. The receiving store enters the received quantity per line; if less, choose a discrepancy reason.

## Tips

- The To store can be any active store of the lab, even outside your branch.
- Free text fields are staff text. Do not enter patient details. Use synthetic names in training.
