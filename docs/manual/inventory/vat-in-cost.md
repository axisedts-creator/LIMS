# VAT in cost

Screen: /inventory/settings, switch "VAT included in cost".

Turn off if your lab can claim purchase VAT back (ask your auditor).

When the switch is on, purchase VAT is part of the item's cost, so it flows into stock value and cost per test. When it is off, VAT is left out of the cost.

Changing the switch applies to future receipts only. Receipts already posted keep their value, so past stock value and past reports do not move.

Ask your auditor before turning it off. Only users who manage master data can change it. See also [cost-per-test.md](cost-per-test.md).
