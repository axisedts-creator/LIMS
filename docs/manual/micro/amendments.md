# Amendments

Once a final report is sent, the culture is locked. To correct it you reopen it for amendment. Earlier reports are never changed or removed.

## Reopening

1. Open the culture and go to the Reports tab. The Reopen for amendment button is available when a final report has been sent.
2. Choose a reason from the list. A free-text note of up to 500 characters is optional. The reason is required; without it you see "Choose a reason for the amendment."
3. Press Reopen. The culture appears on the Amending tab of the worklist.

## Making the correction

Edit the isolates, AST rows or comments that need to change. Then send a new final report. It is stored as a new version of kind Amendment, with the amendment reason shown in the report drawer. The previous report stays readable in the list of versions, and the viewer can open each previous version exactly as it was sent.

## Who may amend

Your role needs the permission to amend results. Without it the buttons are disabled and an explanation is shown. An already approved preliminary report must be followed by a final or an updated preliminary from a pathologist.

## Instrument records

Records marked After final can be applied once the culture is reopened. See [Instrument data matching](instrument-data-matching.md).

## Audit

Reopening, the reason, who sent each version and when are recorded and cannot be edited.
