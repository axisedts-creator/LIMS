# Partner portal manual

This manual covers the B2B partner portal (chunk C13). It has two audiences: people at a partner organisation (clinic, collection centre, hospital) and lab staff who serve them.

All examples use synthetic data only. No real patient, doctor or organisation is named.

## Partner users

- [Manifests, samples and pickups](partner-manifests-samples.md): send samples to the lab and follow them.
- [Reports, price list and statements](partner-reports-billing.md): download released reports and check prices and bills.
- [Doctors, users and account](partner-team.md): manage referring doctors and your team.

## Lab staff

- [Receiving manifests and pickups](staff-receiving.md): receive samples, convert lines to orders, work the pickup queue.
- [Partner administration](staff-administration.md): partner records, doctor mapping and settings.

## Important for the lab: consent

Partner users attest patient consent for every manifest line. The lab cannot verify that attestation, so treat it as a declared statement and not as proof. See the consent sections in the staff pages.
