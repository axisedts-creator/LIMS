# Reports, price list and statements (partner users)

This page is for people at a partner organisation. Examples use synthetic data only.

## Reports

Open `/reports` to see reports the lab has released to your organisation. You need the permission `partners.report.read`. A report that is not released does not appear. Open a report to see its versions; if the lab corrected a report, the newest version is first and older versions stay available.

Downloads are recorded and each user has a daily limit set by the lab. If you reach it, try again the next day or ask the lab to raise the limit.

## Price list

Open `/price-list` to see the tests and panels the lab offers your organisation with the agreed prices. The list is read only. Search by name or code. These are the prices the lab charges your organisation, not the prices you charge patients.

## Statements

Open `/statements` for the monthly statements. You need `partners.statement.read`. Pick a month to see the samples billed in it and the total. A single month is reachable at `/statements/{period_month}`, for example the month written as year-month.

If an amount looks wrong, contact the lab and quote the month and the registration number.

## Handling reports

Reports contain health information. Store them only where your own policy allows and share them only with people entitled to see them.
