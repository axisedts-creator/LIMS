# TAT alerts — user manual

**Screen:** `/quality/tat-alerts`

This page is part of the quality user manual. The same steps appear in the in-app help (press F1 or the ? icon).

## Purpose

See orders and specimens whose turnaround time (TAT) is running late. Alerts have a level: warning, breached or escalated.

## Steps

1. Open TAT alerts. The navigation badge counts breached and escalated alerts not yet acknowledged.
2. Filter by level, segment or department, or show only unacknowledged.
3. Open an alert to see the order, the segment that is late and the time over target.
4. Press Acknowledge. Your name and the time are recorded.

## Shortcuts

- Tab and Shift+Tab move between rows
- Enter opens the focused alert

## Error messages

- "You don't have access to this screen." — your role does not include this screen. There is no Retry button; ask your administrator.
- "Something went wrong" — "Please try again. If it keeps happening, give this reference to support: {request_id}". Press Retry; if it repeats, give the reference to support.

## Tips

- The badge shows 50+ when there are 50 or more.
