# Report templates

Address: `/reporting/templates`. This list has no permission of its own in the route table; opening a template needs `reporting.template.manage` (see [template.md](template.md)).

## Purpose

Report templates control how printed and PDF reports look: page, letterhead, header fields, results layout, signatories, QR code, watermark, and disclaimers. This list shows the templates for your lab.

## Fields

- **Name**: opens the template editor.
- **Family**: Laboratory or Narrative.
- **Published version**: the version new reports use.
- **Draft**: the version being edited, if any. A draft changes nothing until it is published.
- **Active**: whether the template can be used for new reports.

## Steps: change how reports look

1. Open the template by its name.
2. Edit the draft, preview it, and publish it. The editor is described in [template.md](template.md).
3. Come back to this list. The Published version column shows the new number.

## Common questions

- Do old reports change when a template is published? No. Reports already made keep the layout they were made with.
- Why can I see a template but not edit it? Editing needs the template manage permission.

## Shortcuts

None for this screen. Use Tab and Enter to move through the list.
