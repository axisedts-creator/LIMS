Privacy policy
Effective 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Your health information is personal. This policy explains what My Health Now collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number, date of birth, gender, blood group, height and weight; the details of family members you add; your bookings and payment method; and the test results we produce for you. We ask for your address only when you book a home collection.

How we use it

To book and carry out your tests, prepare and deliver your reports, send booking and report notifications, and improve our service. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your reports

You, the family members’ reports you manage under your account, and the Metro Labs staff who process your sample. A doctor sees a report only if you share it with them.

Who we share data with

Payment providers (eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay and card networks) to take payment. Our phlebotomists, for the address and time of a collection. Authorities, only where the law requires it.

Storage and security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Staff access is limited to what their job needs and is logged. Lab records that the law requires us to keep are retained for the period the law sets.

Your choices

You can view and correct your details in Profile, remove a family member at any time, and ask us to delete your account and data through Report a problem. We confirm deletion within 30 days.

Terms & conditions
Effective 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0

By using My Health Now you agree to these terms. The app is operated by Metro Labs, Kathmandu.

Bookings and collection

A booking is confirmed once you receive a booking ID. Please be available at your address during the slot you chose and follow the fasting guidance shown on each test. If you are not available we call twice before marking the visit as missed.

Prices and payment

Prices are in Nepali rupees and include applicable taxes. Member prices apply to bookings made while your membership is active. Home collection is Rs 150 on orders below Rs 1,500 and free otherwise. You can pay by eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, card or cash on collection.

Cancellation and rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 4 hours before your slot. After that, or once our phlebotomist has left for your address, a Rs 200 visit fee applies. Prepaid amounts are refunded to your wallet or card within 3 to 5 working days.

Reports

Reports are released after a pathologist has reviewed them. A report is not a diagnosis. Please discuss your results with your doctor before making any medical decision. Use the QR code on a printed report to confirm it is genuine.

Memberships

A membership lasts 12 months from activation and applies to the people named on your plan. It cannot be transferred, and it is not refundable once a booking has used a member price.

Your account

Keep your phone number up to date and do not share your login. You are responsible for the details you enter for family members, and you confirm you are allowed to book tests for them.
