## Needs lead decision

1. **#3 Upload outcome model.** Should C11 use `accept()`'s `scanState` synchronously, or should C0 add a status/poll method? The same question applies to C6 `process_input_file` (C6 02-TRD l.750 "read scan result from `ctx.uploads`").
2. **#9 REQ-SEC-085 for images.** Are re-encoded PNG/JPEG thumbnails served through the BFF on the staff origin acceptable, or must they come from a separate file origin? This affects C6 and C11.
3. **#26 Change-reason ownership.** Should C11 keep only the global set mirrored from C5, or allow tenant codes that must also exist in C5?
4. **#7 Post-sign-out edit set.** Which data (gross, parts, images, molecular) may change while a revision/amendment draft is open, and can images be uploaded for addenda?
5. **#8 Staff directory access.** Should there be a new `ctx.core.memberships.search` (C0 CR), or should A12 be redefined on the existing `memberships.list`?
6. **Plain text vs rich text (no row; noted for the record).** 02-TRD §4.6 chooses plain text, labelled DECISION. research/01 §2.4 (higher precedence than chunk docs) describes "rich-text templates with placeholders". The choice is reasoned (smaller XSS surface; 03-IC §6.8 `*_text` columns), but because it departs from the research recommendation the lead should confirm it explicitly.
