## Needs lead decision

1. **#5 `smartreport.summary_not_ready` semantics.** Choose between "port returns `status:'draft'`" (C14 design, 03-IC case 10) and "port throws `summary_not_ready` for staff callers" (03-IC `@throws` after A4.6). Fix 03-IC, the C0 fake and contract suite, and C6's error handling to match.
2. **#3 Device actors and `smartreport.*` RLS.** Choose between adding `current_actor_type() <> 'device'` to every smartreport policy, or accepting device visibility and changing the C14 test and statement. Consider whether the same applies program-wide, since `is_staff_or_system()` admits devices for every `__tenant` table.
3. **#2 Global deny list vs mandatory non-diagnostic wording.** Decide whether `diagnos` stays a global, unremovable term. If it does, the starter texts and any disclaimer must avoid the word.
4. **#1 Supersede chain.** Choose a deferrable FK or a guarded `superseded→superseded` transition. Either is fine; C0-0B needs one answer.
5. **#4 FrontdeskPort consumer CR.** Add C14 as a FrontdeskPort consumer and choose the demographics purpose for queue names (`lab_display` reuse vs a new C14 purpose).
