## Needs lead decision

1. **#11 Missed-visit sweep vs late offline uploads.** Either the sweep must not unassign visits that a device has pulled that day, or a new transition `unassigned → in_progress` restoring the original assignee is needed. This choice changes the state machine and C0's transition trigger.
2. **#13 Re-auth fallback while the biometric/device-credential plugin is not pinned.** The current fallback (online password login) breaks offline work. Options: an offline app PIN (new secure-storage secret and its brute-force rules), or making CR-C17-010 a hard precondition before the app ships.
3. **#1 Handover visibility at the receiving centre.** The proposed fix copies barcode and specimen type into `handover_items` (more P2 data stored server-side) and moves discrepancy flags to a worker job. The alternative is to make receivers members of the home-collection centre, which widens their access to all visits. This is a data-minimisation trade-off.
4. **#16 Payment-method codes.** Validating C17 settings against C4's methods needs a new C4 read path (port method or published view). Whether eSewa and Khalti references are mandatory in the field, when C4's seed says they are not, is a product decision.
5. **#15 SMS templates.** The two SMS features need a C6 CR. The alternative is to drop them from v1 and remove both tenant switches.
6. **#35 Tenant-editable reason codes.** Keep them (new endpoint and UI) or drop `cs_reasons` from v1.
7. **#9 Per-phlebotomist daily visit cap.** Confirm 60 as the cap across all of a phlebotomist's routes, and that assignment refuses past it rather than the snapshot truncating.
